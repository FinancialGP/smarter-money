SMARTER $

A New Zealand classroom game about income, tax, expenses, markets, debt, and building passive income — over 10 years (120 months).

Win goal: Financial Freedom — passive income ≥ total expenses (P÷E ≥ 100%) at a payday. The sooner, the better. Then see your summary or keep playing to month 120.

Each month: monthly index update (Gold/Silver, Bitcoin and US Index ETF prices move), then 4 events in order — 1 Market news · 2 Opportunity · 3 Life / spending · 4 Wildcard — then Next Month = payday. Get sick? No other events that month.

Choose Difficulty

Every month: index update (all 3 ETFs) → 1 market news → 2 deal → 3 life/spending → 4 wildcard (deal or life). Harder levels mean more spending, fewer deals, more illness and a tougher start. Each deck (64 cards; market 76) is shuffled; a card comes back only after the whole deck is used. Permanent cost rises happen at most once per game.
