SMARTER $ — Debt Paydown

A simpler New Zealand classroom game: start already in debt, protect your cashflow, and pay down consumer debts month by month (NZD).

Primary goal: become consumer-debt-free (everything except the home loan).
Stretch goal: become fully debt-free including the mortgage.
